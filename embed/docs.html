Embed Echo Radar WX

Put the live weather radar on your own website, free. It is the same radar as the Echo Radar app: the full-resolution scan from the nearest NOAA NEXRAD radar, straight from NOAA's Level 2 data, with active National Weather Service warnings on top. Visitors can pan, zoom, tap a radar to switch to it, and tap a warning for details. Zoomed out to the whole country, it shows the national radar mosaic. No account or key needed: pick your settings, copy a snippet, paste it into your page.

With the script tag, put each setting on the element as data-lat="33.75" and so on, plus
    data-height. With the iframe, add them to the address: https://api.echoradarwx.com/embed?lat=33.75&lon=-84.39&zoom=6.


  
Good to know

  
Radar data: NOAA NEXRAD Level 2 and MRMS. Warnings: National Weather Service. The radar picks up each new scan
    by itself (radars scan every two to six minutes) and the warnings refresh every minute, so the page never needs reloading.
    Zoomed in, the full-resolution radar is downloaded by each visitor's browser (around 10 to 30 MB per scan), so it suits
    a desktop or Wi-Fi more than a phone on a slow connection. Please keep the Echo Radar WX badge visible. For full
    single-site radar, velocity, alerts to your phone and more, use the app at echoradarwx.com.
    Never rely on any one website in life-threatening weather: follow your local NWS office and emergency officials.
